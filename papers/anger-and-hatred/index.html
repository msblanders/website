The evolutionary logic of anger and hatred

An empirical test

Mitchell Landers · Aaron Sell · Coltan Scrivner · Anthony Lopez

Read the publication Read the article PDF

Overview

In a preregistered recall study, 725 participants in the United States and United Kingdom described experiences of anger or hatred. The study tested predictions about the goals associated with each emotion.

Based on self-reported goals, anger was associated with seeking better treatment, while hatred was associated with distancing from or harming the target.

  • Preregistered research
  • Recall design
  • United States and United Kingdom
  • N = 725
