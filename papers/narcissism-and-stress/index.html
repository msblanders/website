Narcissism and stress

Cortisol reactivity to psychosocial stress in vulnerable and grandiose narcissists: An exploratory study

Javier I. Borráz-León · Alena Spreitzer · Coltan Scrivner · Mitchell Landers · Royce Lee · Dario Maestripieri

Read PDFDownload PDFPublisher page

Overview

This exploratory study examined whether vulnerable and grandiose narcissism are associated with different responses to social evaluation. Forty-seven participants completed the Trier Social Stress Test, provided saliva samples for cortisol measurement, and answered personality questionnaires.

Participants with higher vulnerable narcissism scores showed stronger cortisol and emotional responses than those with higher grandiose narcissism scores. The small, exploratory sample calls for confirmation in larger and more diverse samples.

  • N = 47
  • Trier Social Stress Test
  • Salivary cortisol
  • Personality questionnaires
