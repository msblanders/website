Shame

An evolutionary account of shame and the threat of social devaluation

Mitchell Landers · Daniel Sznycer · Laith Al-Shawaf

Read PDFDownload PDFPublisher page

Overview

Why does shame occur, and what does it accomplish? This chapter reviews an evolutionary account in which shame helps people anticipate and manage the costs of being devalued by others. It considers how shame can discourage costly choices, limit the spread of damaging information, and coordinate responses when social devaluation occurs.

The chapter brings together theory and evidence on the information threat account of shame, including its implications for emotion, behavior, and social evaluation.
