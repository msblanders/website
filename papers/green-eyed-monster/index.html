Green-eyed monster or green-eyed mirage?

A new procedure for telling when begrudging others' success is or is not envy

Mitchell Landers · Alex Shaw

Read the publication View the preprint

Overview

Across eight studies (N = 1,507), this paper develops a third-party criterion for testing whether an observed effect is attributable specifically to envy. It checks whether the same elicitor affects uninvolved observers, providing a comparison for interpreting the effect.

Deservingness effects failed the criterion; audience-valuation effects passed. The criterion addresses whether a particular effect is envy-specific, rather than establishing what an instrument measures in general.

  • Construct validity
