Are self-conscious emotions about the self?

Testing competing theories of shame and guilt across two disparate cultures

Mitchell Landers · Daniel Sznycer · Patrick K. Durkee

Mitchell Landers and Daniel Sznycer contributed equally (joint first authors).

Read the publication Read the author-shared PDF

Overview

This paper tests competing theories of shame and guilt using experimental manipulations and structural equation models with participants in the United States and India (N = 853). The research uses preregistered tests to distinguish the theories' predictions across two cultural settings.

  • Cross-cultural research
  • Experimental design
  • Structural equation modeling
  • N = 853
