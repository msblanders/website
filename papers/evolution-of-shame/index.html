The evolution of shame and its display

A theoretical review of shame, social devaluation, and emotional display

Mitchell Landers · Daniel Sznycer

Read the open-access article Read on PubMed Central

Overview

This theoretical review synthesizes evidence on shame and its characteristic display. It examines shame as a response to the threat of social devaluation and considers how posture, gaze, and speech may help limit reputational damage.

  • Theory development
  • Social emotions
